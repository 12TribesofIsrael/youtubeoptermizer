**Decided 2026-07-16.** Build "From Eden to Timbuktu" as a **single ~86-min video**, not eight
13-min parts. Part One (`vnkEWbCVMTA`, public since 2026-07-03) stays as a teaser/funnel.

**Why — the analytics forced it:**
- YPP reapply is **blocked on eligibility, not the date**: 1,393 / 3,000 valid public watch hours.
  The Shorts path (3M views/90d) is fantasy — the channel did 72K Shorts views in a *year*, 7.8K
  in the last 90 days.
- **YPP watch hours EXCLUDE Shorts.** Qualifying hours are long-form only.
- **One video carries ~80% of all qualifying watch time**: "The Prophecy Revealed" (1h29m) =
  ~1,100 of 1,389 hrs/yr, stable and search-driven, ~100–150 hrs/month, *growing* not decaying.
- Watch hours = duration × views. A 13.6-min part **cannot** be an engine at any retention.
  Eden Part One: 47 views in 2 weeks. The 2h Psalms video gets 86 views and still out-earns it.
- 86 min ≈ Prophecy Revealed's 89 min — the proven engine profile, and the narration for it
  already existed.

**Realistic timeline:** roughly one more Prophecy-Revealed-caliber evergreen, matured ~12 months
→ **2027 approval**, not summer 2026. No deadline pressure: the Reapply button is greyed until
eligible, so a premature attempt can't be burned.

**Strike reason was "unsatisfying or off-putting content"** (the AI-slop flag) — a researched
documentary with original prose and real museum artifacts is the answer; more short AI clips is
not.
