The first great cities rose there. The first written codes of law, the first schools, the first works of epic literature — every one of these began among the dark-skinned people between the two rivers. - **p14 | ARCHIVAL | golden-ambient**

The idea of sorting all of humanity into a *white* column and a *black* column the way we do today — that did not exist in the ancient world. So when an ancient writer doesn't stop to tell you a man was Black, it is often because, in that part of the world, in that age, it did not need saying. It was the assumption."
