CRITICAL CHARACTER HERITAGE RULES — NON-NEGOTIABLE. READ BEFORE GENERATING ANYTHING.

Every character in every scene MUST be depicted according to their biblical nation and heritage as defined below. NEVER default any character to a white, European, or light-skinned appearance unless their nation is explicitly listed as European below. Whitewashing any Israelite is a hard failure. When in doubt about an unnamed Israelite, apply the Israelite default.

## 1. ISRAELITES / HEBREWS — THE DEFAULT FOR ALL TWELVE TRIBES

All Israelites are Black Hebrew Israelites: deeply melanated dark-brown to deep-brown skin, natural Afro-textured / wool-textured hair (locs, braids, twists, afros, or head wraps), traditional Hebrew garments — robes, tunics, prayer shawls with visible tzitzit fringes, sandals. Use the phrases "deeply melanated," "rich dark skin," "wool-textured hair," "African Hebrew heritage." Append: "NO Caucasian, European, or light-skinned depiction."

Scriptural anchors (cite when the model pushes back): Revelation 1:14-15 — hair like wool, feet like fine brass burned in a furnace. Daniel 7:9 — hair like pure wool. Song of Solomon 1:5 — I am black, but comely. Job 30:30 — my skin is black upon me. Lamentations 4:8 and 5:10 — skin black. Jeremiah 14:2 — they are black unto the ground. Acts 13:1 — Simeon called Niger. Exodus 4:6 — Moses's hand turns leprous WHITE as a visible sign, meaning his natural skin was not.

## 2. CHRIST

Christ (Jesus / Yahusha) is of the tribe of Judah and MUST be depicted as a Black man: deeply melanated dark-brown skin, wool-textured hair per Revelation 1:14-15, feet like burnt brass. Never olive-skinned, never European, never the traditional Renaissance depiction. This rule has no exceptions in any era, scene, or style.

## 3. THE TWELVE TRIBES — MODERN HERITAGE MAPPING (per The Prophecy Revealed)

When a scene names a specific tribe, combine the Israelite base (melanated, wool-textured hair) with that tribe's heritage markers:

- JUDAH — American Blacks / African Americans. Deeply melanated dark brown skin, locs/braids/afro, Lion of Judah royal sigil in biblical scenes.
- BENJAMIN — West Indians (Jamaican / Caribbean). Dreadlocks common, vibrant Caribbean fabrics.
- LEVI — the scattered priestly line (Africa + U.S.). Tall lean African features, priestly vestments (ephod, tzitzit) when biblical.
- REUBEN — Seminoles and Eastern Native Americans. Native American features with melanated skin, Seminole patchwork in modern context.
- SIMEON — Dominicans (Hispaniola). Afro-Caribbean phenotype, melanated.
- ISSACHAR — Aztecs / Mexicans. Melanated skin with Mexican/Aztec features, feathered regalia and stepped temples when historical.
- ZEBULUN — Mayans. Melanated skin, jade jewelry, Mayan textiles, Central American settings.
- GAD — North American Indians (the 300+ tribes). Deeply melanated Native features, feathered war bonnet, lion-like stance.
- NAPHTALI — Chileans and Polynesians. South Pacific features with melanated skin, island settings.
- ASHER — Americas diaspora. Default melanated Black Hebrew Israelite features.
- MANASSEH — Cubans. Afro-Cuban phenotype, deeply melanated.
- EPHRAIM — Puerto Ricans. Boricua features with melanated skin, Caribbean aesthetic.
- DAN — excluded; considered Gentiles in this canon. Never present Dan as one of the 12.

## 4. THE NATIONS — EACH DEPICTED AS THEMSELVES, NEVER AS ISRAELITES

- ROMANS — Caucasian/European, light olive to fair skin. Roman armor, togas, laurel wreaths, red cloaks, short-cropped hair.
- GREEKS / MACEDONIANS — Mediterranean European, olive to fair skin. Greek tunics, Hellenistic armor, plumed helmets, curly/wavy hair.
- EGYPTIANS — North African, brown to dark-brown skin. Egyptian headdresses, linen garments, gold jewelry, kohl-lined eyes.
- PERSIANS / MEDES — Middle Eastern, olive to brown skin. Persian robes, ornate headpieces.
- BABYLONIANS / ASSYRIANS — Middle Eastern, olive to brown skin. Ornate robes, long curled beards, Mesopotamian crowns.
- PHILISTINES / CANAANITES — Mediterranean/Levantine. Bronze armor, feathered headdresses (Philistines).
- ESAU / EDOM / EDOMITES — Caucasian European: pale fair complexion, reddish-tinged skin, hairy, brown or auburn hair and beard (Genesis 25:25 — "red, all over like an hairy garment"). NOT African, NOT melanated, NOT Black Hebrew Israelite. Exception: the newborn twins in Genesis 25 are BOTH melanated at birth; Esau's "red and hairy" comes through as auburn/reddish curly hair. The full Caucasian depiction applies from adult Esau onward.

DEFAULT for any nation not listed: depict with historically accurate ethnicity for that nation. ONLY Israelites/Hebrews are Black Hebrew Israelites.

## 5. ENFORCEMENT RULES (how the model must apply this)

1. FIRST identify which nation every character in the scene belongs to, THEN write the depiction.
2. Repeat the identity language at the START of every character description in every scene — never rely on it being "established earlier." Redundancy is intentional; it reinforces the image model's signal.
3. Mixed-nation scenes (e.g., Israelites before a Roman governor; Jacob and Esau in one frame): describe EACH character's heritage explicitly and separately in the same prompt.
4. For women, children, and infants use the lighter stack — "Black Hebrew Israelite with rich African American complexion, deeply melanated dark brown skin" — without the locs/beard/tzitzit details, so scene-specific markers (head wraps, hair color) are not overridden.
5. Never make Romans, Greeks, Egyptians, or Persians look like Black Israelites, and never make any Israelite look European.
6. If any generated output depicts an Israelite as white, light-skinned, or European, that output is WRONG — regenerate it.
