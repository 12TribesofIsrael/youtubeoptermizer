In ANY script, doc, or narration produced for the AI Bible Gospels channel, the transfer of Isaac's blessing from Esau to Jacob must be framed as **God's pre-ordained will being fulfilled through Rebekah's obedience** — NEVER as theft, trickery, robbery, or stealing.

**The locked narrative:**
- Genesis 25:23 — God spoke to Rebekah BEFORE the twins were born: "the elder shall serve the younger." The blessing was Jacob's by divine decree before either boy drew breath.
- Esau **despised** his birthright (Gen 25:34) by trading it to Jacob for a bowl of red pottage. He forfeited the covenant willingly.
- Rebekah was acting in OBEDIENCE to the word God had given her. Isaac was attempting to bless Esau against God's prophecy; Rebekah moved to ensure God's word stood.
- Therefore the blessing was **transferred, fulfilled, ordained** — never stolen.

**Words to AVOID in scripts and narration:**
- "Jacob stole the blessing"
- "The blessing was stolen"
- "Rebekah tricked Isaac"
- "Jacob deceived his father"
- "Theft" / "stolen" / "trick" / "deception" in this context
- Section headers like "THE THEFT" — use "THE PROPHECY FULFILLED" or "THE BLESSING TRANSFERRED"

**Words to USE instead:**
- "The blessing was transferred / fulfilled / poured out as God ordained"
- "Rebekah moved in obedience to God's word"
- "The Most High had already chosen Jacob from the womb"
- "The prophecy of Genesis 25:23 was fulfilled"
- "Esau forfeited his covenant by despising his birthright"

**Why this matters:** This is the BHI theological reading of Genesis 27. Calling it "theft" reduces a God-ordained covenant transfer to human deception, which undercuts the entire foundation of Jacob's lineage = the chosen people. Channel content lives or dies on this framing — if Jacob "stole" the blessing, then Black Hebrew Israelites are descended from a thief; if God ORDAINED the transfer, BHI are descended from God's chosen.
