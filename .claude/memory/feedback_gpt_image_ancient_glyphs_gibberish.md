gpt-image-1 sets **English words in Latin capitals flawlessly** on the brand card (proven again
across the Eden→Timbuktu cards). Ask it for **ancient letterforms** — Phoenician glyphs, Greek
letters, a "letters morphing Phoenician → Greek → Latin" chain — and it produces
authentic-*looking* nonsense. The Eden p3_6 card rendered "ᴜΓDƐ→ΛΟΓΒΑ WORDS" on 2026-07-16.

The danger is that the gibberish looks plausible at a glance, so it survives review unless
someone actually reads it.

**How to apply:** carry the idea with English words the model can spell. p3_6 became a chain of
four gold serif words — PHOENICIAN → GREEK → LATIN → ENGLISH — and rendered clean (note: it
dropped the 4th word; expect 3-4 items max per horizontal chain). The aleph/beth punchline card
(`ALEPH · BETH → ALPHABET`) renders perfectly because it's all Latin caps. Let the narration
carry the ancient-script detail; the card carries the English.
